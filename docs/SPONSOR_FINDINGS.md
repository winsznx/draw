Labels: `UNVERIFIED` (read, not run), `LOCAL_VERIFIED` (ran on LocalNet), `DEVNET_VERIFIED` (ran on DevNet). Daml Script results under `dpm test` are recorded as `SCRIPT_VERIFIED`, which is weaker than `LOCAL_VERIFIED` because no participant or real token is involved. `DOC_VERIFIED` means the quoted text was checked at the source on the date given, and nothing was run. `NOT_USED` means read, and DRAW doesn't call it. `OBSERVED` means the DevNet node behaved this way once, checked by hand or by one probe check, and DRAW doesn't rely on it. `PROBED` means a one-off script of ours called the DevNet node, wrote no evidence file, and its output is kept outside the repository. `REPORTED` means a probe result written down from the session at the time, with no saved output.
