# Portfolio novelty

This hackathon process record covers one check: whether DRAW is new compared with the builder's own earlier products, and the framing rule that came out of it. The product is in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) and the proof in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).

## Verdict

Checked on 2026-10-02 against the builder's six most recent products, from their READMEs and top-level docs. None of them models a fund call or per-LP obligations, and none uses Canton. The proof page, the seeded campaign and the database comparison follow a format the builder has used before. So the verdict was ADJACENT: the product is new, and the way its proof is presented is familiar.

## The framing rule

- The README and the demo open on the fund: a call, three LPs, two paid and one didn't, and what's deployable. The proof page comes last and isn't shown in the first 30 seconds.
- The app says funded, settle, notice and receipt.
- The read-back step appears inside a receipt.

The 10-second check that comes from this rule is in [JUDGE_NOTE.md](JUDGE_NOTE.md): if a judge remembers a verifier, the story failed.
