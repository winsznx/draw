# Rubric to artifact map

This hackathon process record maps each of the six judging criteria to where DRAW answers it, for judges who score by criterion. The product is on one page in the [README](../README.md) and step by step in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md), the proof is in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md), and the full reading order and a glossary are in the [docs index](README.md).

The criteria and "What judges look for" are quoted word for word from the HackCanton Season 3 rules (https://api.appsfactory.cc/api/hackathons/season-3, "Judging Criteria", read 2026-10-06). [claims.yaml](../claims.yaml) is the ceiling for every line. DevNet in the tables below means the HackCanton shared node, where DRAW's backend login signs for every demo seat. Payments there settle in Canton Coin from the DevNet faucet, and dollar figures are the scenario.

## Track 3 expected output

Track 3, Investment Infrastructure: Funds, DAOs & Governance Tools, is the track DRAW enters, confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-07 ([TRACK_PROOF_MODALITY.md](TRACK_PROOF_MODALITY.md)). The expected output is quoted from the same rules page.

| Expected output | DRAW |
|---|---|
| An MVP for fund management, DAO governance or capital coordination | The capital-call lifecycle for one fund: commitments, calls, payment, settlement, overdue, review and waive |
| Role-based workflows (e.g., managers, investors, voters) | A fund manager seat and three LP seats, each reading the ledger as its own party, plus an unrelated party that sees nothing. On DevNet one backend client acts for every seat, and an LP can also sign in with its own HackCanton login and act with its own token |
| A demonstration of transparency, auditability and operational logic | Settlement receipts with 14 checks, a note on every review, reopen, release and waive, the call timeline, and the proof page that links each result file |
| A brief describing the target users and operational use case | [BUSINESS_BRIEF.md](BUSINESS_BRIEF.md) |

The Track 3 wishlist asks for "A Daml workflow for the full lifecycle of private fund subscriptions, capital calls, distributions and redemptions with on-ledger NAV records". DRAW covers the capital-call part of that lifecycle, with commitment acceptance in front of it. Distributions, redemptions and NAV records aren't built.
